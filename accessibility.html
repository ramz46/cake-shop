Accessibility Statement

Maison Élysée is dedicated to providing an elegant, barrier-free digital environment where every visitor, regardless of physical ability, device, or assistive technology, can explore our haute pâtisserie creations with dignity and ease.

Conformance Standard

Our website is actively engineered to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA criteria.

1. Keyboard Navigation & Focus Management

Every interactive element across our digital studio—including navigation dropdowns, modal lightboxes, flavor customizers, and contact forms—is completely operable via standard keyboard commands (Tab, Enter, Space, and Esc). Visible, high-contrast gold focus rings guide keyboard users without ambiguity.

2. Sensory & Contrast Modes

Visitors can toggle between our curated Light and Dark aesthetics using the header controls. Both palettes maintain a verified color contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for body copy and 3:1 for large display headlines, ensuring optimal readability for individuals with low vision or color sensitivity.

3. Reduced Motion Compatibility

For individuals sensitive to vestibular movement, our website automatically respects operating system motion preferences (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce). When enabled, all parallax scroll effects, 3D card tilt simulations, and decorative gold dust particle animations are instantly silenced.

4. Screen Reader Optimization & Alt Descriptions

All imagery featuring our bespoke wedding cakes, handcrafted sugar petals, and dessert tables includes rich, descriptive alternative text conveying the design's structural tiers, materials, and botanical garnishes. Structural sections use native HTML5 landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>) and ARIA attributes.

5. Accommodations & Feedback

If you encounter any barrier while browsing our site or wish to request an accessible format of our seasonal flavor collection, please contact our digital concierge at accessibility@maisonelysee.com or via telephone at +33 (0)1 42 68 55 00.
