Bespoke Design Process

The Bespoke Commission Journey

❧ ✦ ❧

From initial flavor flights in our private studio to on-site white-glove staging on your wedding day, every creation is managed with meticulous culinary precision.

01 Initial Flight

Private Tasting Studio

Sample 8 bespoke cake and mousse combinations alongside sommelier-selected French champagnes in our private tasting suite.

02 Visual Couture

Architectural Sketch

Chef Geneviève Moreau produces hand-drawn watercolor sketches harmonizing with your wedding gown, linens, and floral arrangements.

03 Artisanal Craft

Sugar Botanical Sculpting

Each petal, stamen, and leaf is hand-rolled and tinted from sugar paste, achieving delicate botanical realism and gold foil accents.

04 Climate Transit

Chilled Logistics

Internal food-grade stainless armatures and dual-refrigerated courier transit safeguard structural integrity in any climate.

05 Grand Reveal

Day-Of White-Glove Staging

Our master pastry chefs assemble, level, and place the final sugar floristry on-site at your venue two hours prior to reception.

The Studio Philosophy

Where Fine Artistry Meets Sensory Indulgence

❧ ✦ ❧

We believe a wedding cake should not only serve as the crowning visual sculpture of your reception room, but also deliver an unforgettable culinary revelation that lingers in memory for a lifetime.

Founded by Chef Geneviève Moreau following a decadelong career in Michelin-starred Parisian kitchens, Maison Élysée rejects mass shortcuts. We source wild blackberries from alpine valleys, cold-pressed French Normandy butter, organic Tahitian vanilla orchids, and Grand Cru single-origin chocolate from Valrhona.

Geneviève Moreau

Founder & Master Pastry Chef

🌿

Botanical Sugar Floristry

Every petal, stamen, and leaf is sculpted individually by hand from artisanal sugar paste, reproducing organic English garden blooms with botanical accuracy.

🏛️

Architectural Cantilevers

Internal food-grade stainless armatures engineered for zero-gravity cantilever tiers, floating geometry, and monumental 7-tier stability in all climates.

🍯

Terroir & Michelin Pedigree

Zero premixes, artificial oils, or syrups. Only pure Normandy churned butter, single-estate Grand Cru cocoa, and cold-pressed fresh fruit reductions.
