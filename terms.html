Terms of Commission

These terms define our mutual commitment when commissioning an architectural gateau, dessert table installation, or private tasting experience with Maison Élysée.

Commission Exclusivity

To uphold uncompromising haute couture quality, Chef Geneviève Moreau limits the studio to no more than two grand wedding commissions per calendar weekend.

1. Retainer & Payment Schedule

A non-refundable 50% retainer fee and signed commission agreement are required to reserve your wedding date on our master calendar. The remaining balance, including any final guest count adjustments or rental pedestals, is due strictly 30 days prior to your celebration date.

2. Design Approvals & Modifications

Initial watercolor sketches and sugar botanical color palettes are presented during or following your tasting salon appointment. Any major architectural tier additions, floral motif overhauls, or flavor changes must be finalized at least 45 days prior to the wedding date to allow custom sourcing of Grand Cru ingredients.

3. White-Glove Transport & On-Site Delivery

Every wedding commission is escorted in our custom climate-controlled refrigerated transport vans. Our setup team arrives 2 to 3 hours prior to guest arrival to lock structural internal dowels, place fresh sugar blossoms, and perform final gilding. The venue or wedding planner must provide:

  • A completely level, sturdy, and weight-tested display table (minimum 45 kg load capacity).
  • A climate-controlled environment shielded from direct sunlight, outdoor rainfall, or direct heating elements.
  • Direct catering access for our delivery master to perform on-site assembly.

4. Postponements & Force Majeure

Should an unforeseen event require date postponement, your initial retainer will transfer in full to your new date within a 12-month period, subject to studio calendar availability. Cancellations requested less than 30 days prior to the event are non-refundable due to pre-ordered specialized botanicals and custom sugar modeling work.

5. Dietary Restrictions & Allergens

While we accommodate gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-conscious commissions with specialized separate sanitization protocols, our Paris atelier actively processes tree nuts, wheat, eggs, and dairy. Clients must inform our team of severe guest allergies during the initial tasting consultation.
