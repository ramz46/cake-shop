Customer Assistance

Boutique & Ordering Questions

How long do your macarons and fresh entremets stay fresh? +

Our macarons maintain optimal texture for up to 5 days when stored refrigerated inside their sealed box. Single entremets are freshly prepared and best enjoyed within 36 hours.

Do you offer gluten-free and allergen-sensitive selections? +

Yes! All our traditional macarons are naturally made with 100% almond flour without wheat gluten. We also offer certified nut-free and dairy-free bespoke boxes upon 24 hours advance notice.

What is the lead time for same-day boutique orders? +

Daily boutique items placed before 14:00 can be dispatched via our Paris refrigerated courier for same-day evening delivery. Boutique pickup at 740 Grand Avenue is ready within 45 minutes.
