The Essential Wedding Cake Size & Portion Guide

Henri Laurent Head of Structural Design & Production

Scale architecture: Balancing visual commanding presence in grand ballrooms with practical catering portions.

One of the most frequent challenges couples encounter when planning a luxury reception is reconciling structural visual scale with actual guest numbers. A three-tier cake can easily look diminutive in a high-ceilinged palace ballroom, while an eight-tier gateau might generate excessive leftover servings for an intimate vineyard gathering.

Coffee Servings vs. Plated Dessert Servings

In luxury catering, portion calculations depend strictly on how the cake is presented to guests:

  • Plated Dessert Portion (1" × 2" × 5" tall): Served seated with coulis, edible flowers, and gelato as the formal dessert course. Plan for 100% of guest headcount.
  • Canapé / Coffee Portion (1" × 1" × 5" tall): Served roaming alongside an espresso bar and late-night dessert buffet. Plan for 70% to 80% of guest headcount.
“A monumental cake should command the grand ballroom, yet every slice that reaches the table must be cut with pristine geometric precision.”

The Tier-to-Guest Ratio Blueprint

2 Tiers (6" + 8" Diameters) Height: 28–32 cm • Perfect for intimate dinners & chateau elopements
40 – 55 Guests
3 Tiers (6" + 8" + 10" Diameters) Height: 45–50 cm • The gold standard classical wedding proportion
75 – 110 Guests
4 Tiers (6" + 8" + 10" + 12" Diameters) Height: 62–70 cm • Majestic silhouette for estate & ballroom galas
140 – 190 Guests
5 Tiers (6" + 8" + 10" + 12" + 14" Diameters) Height: 80–92 cm • Monumental royal centerpiece requiring internal doweling
220 – 280 Guests
6+ Tiers Architectural Colonnade Height: Over 1.2 meters • Custom sugar pillars and floating illusion tiers
300+ Guests

The Secret of Architectural Faux Tiers

Couples often ask: "Can we commission a towering 6-tier sculpture if we only have 80 guests?" The answer is an emphatic yes. Through the discreet incorporation of architectural food-grade polystyrene structural dummy tiers—iced, piped, and gilded identically to real cake tiers—we produce monumental visual impact while keeping the edible base tiers perfectly tailored to your guest count without wasteful excess.

White-Glove Cutting Protocol

Maison Élysée provides your head catering team with a detailed architectural cutting map illustrating the internal acrylic dowel anchors, tier removal order, and slice portion guide to ensure prompt, elegant presentation at your reception.
