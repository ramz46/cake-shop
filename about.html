The Studio Story

Couture Cake Craftsmanship Rooted in Fine Pastry Art

Founded on the belief that a wedding cake should be an architectural marvel on the outside and an exquisite culinary triumph upon the first bite, Maison Élysée operates at the intersection of couture design and fine pastry art.

Each commission begins as an intimate dialogue between our pastry artists and our couples. We reject cookie-cutter formulas and pre-made fillings, handcrafting every sponge, curd, fruit preserve, and botanical sugar flower in our private studio.

The Collaboration

From Watercolor Sketch to Grand Reception

Our streamlined 5-step journey ensures your wedding confection is an effortless, deeply pleasurable planning experience.

01

The Creative Brief & Date Reservation

We review your wedding date, venue architecture, guest count, and floral moodboards. A $350 initial studio deposit secures your weekend date on our limited production calendar.

02

The Private Tasting Experience

Join us in our private studio for a curated tasting of six bespoke cake flavor pairings accompanied by sommelier-selected Premier Cru Champagne or botanical sparkling infusions.

03

Architectural Design & Watercolor Rendering

Chef Geneviève produces a hand-painted watercolor architectural elevation of your cake, detailing tier ratios, bas-relief motifs, sugar flower placement, and linen color coordinates.

04

Artisanal Baking & Sugar Craft

Sugar florals and bas-relief lacework are sculpted weeks in advance. Sponges are baked completely fresh 48 hours prior to your wedding using pure organic Normandy butter and Grand Cru chocolate.

05

White-Glove Delivery & On-Site Styling

Delivered in climate-controlled transport vehicles. Our master pastry decorator personally installs the cake on your reception table, adds final floral touches, and coordinates with your caterer for precision slicing.
